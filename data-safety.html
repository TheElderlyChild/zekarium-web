Steps to Delete Your Account and Data

1. Login

Login to your Zekarium account using your credentials.

2. Dashboard

Navigate to your account dashboard and click 'profile'.

3. Edit Profile

Click Edit Profile on the Profile Page

4. Click Delete

On the Profile Page, Click the Delete button to initiate the deletion process.

5. Confirm

Click confirm to permanently delete your account and all associated data. This action is irreversible.
