Zekarium

Information Security Policy

1. Purpose

This Policy establishes the principles and controls adopted by Zekarium to safeguard all information assets, particularly customer financial and personal data, against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • All employees, directors, contractors, and partners of Zekarium
  • All systems, applications, databases, and infrastructure
  • All data processed, stored, or transmitted by Zekarium

3. Objectives

Zekarium aims to:

  • Protect customer and business data at all times
  • Ensure secure lending operations and transactions
  • Maintain trust and platform integrity
  • Comply with applicable Nigerian laws and fintech regulations

4. Regulatory Compliance

Zekarium shall comply with:

  • Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)
  • Applicable guidelines of the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)
  • Any other relevant financial, cybersecurity, and data protection laws

5. Information Classification

All data shall be classified as:

  • Public – Non-sensitive information
  • Internal – Restricted to employees
  • Confidential – Customer, financial, and operational data
  • Highly Confidential – Credit data, identity records, financial credentials

6. Access Control

  • Access is granted strictly on a need-to-know and least privilege basis
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) shall be enforced for critical systems
  • User access rights must be reviewed periodically
  • Immediate revocation of access upon termination or role change

7. Data Protection & Privacy

  • All sensitive data must be encrypted at rest and in transit
  • Customer data must only be used for legitimate lending and operational purposes
  • Data collection shall be minimal and purpose-specific
  • Data retention policies must be defined and enforced
  • Secure disposal methods must be used for obsolete data

8. Application & Platform Security

  • Zekarium shall implement secure coding practices
  • Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing shall be conducted
  • APIs and integrations must be secured and authenticated
  • Continuous monitoring for fraud and suspicious activity

9. Network & Infrastructure Security

  • Firewalls, intrusion detection/prevention systems must be in place
  • Systems must be regularly patched and updated
  • Cloud infrastructure must follow industry security best practices
  • Secure configuration standards must be enforced

10. Incident Response

  • All security incidents must be reported immediately
  • A formal Incident Response Plan shall be maintained
  • Breaches involving personal data shall be reported in line with NDPR requirements
  • Root cause analysis and corrective actions must be implemented

11. Third-Party Risk Management

  • All partners (e.g., payment processors, data providers) must meet Zekarium’s security standards
  • Data sharing must be governed by contracts and confidentiality agreements
  • Periodic vendor risk assessments shall be conducted

12. Employee Responsibilities

All personnel must:

  • Protect confidential information at all times
  • Not share passwords or access credentials
  • Report suspicious activities or breaches
  • Comply fully with this Policy

13. Training & Awareness

  • Mandatory security awareness training for all staff
  • Periodic updates on phishing, fraud, and cyber risks
  • Role-based training for technical and sensitive roles

14. Business Continuity & Backup

  • Regular backups of critical systems and data
  • Disaster recovery plans must be in place and tested
  • Systems must be resilient to minimize downtime

15. Enforcement & Sanctions

  • Violations of this Policy may result in disciplinary action, termination, or legal consequences
  • Zekarium reserves the right to monitor system usage to ensure compliance

16. Policy Review

This Policy shall be reviewed at least annually or upon significant changes in operations, technology, or regulation.

17. Approval

This Policy is approved by the Management of Zekarium and is effective immediately upon adoption.
